About

Built by a store. Made for stores.

We're Ashfaq Ali Zardari Store — a Pakistan-based business that makes tools we wish we'd had.

Our story

Ashfaq Ali Zardari Store started as a small shop with big ambitions. We quickly learned that off-the-shelf accounting software didn't fit the way we — and thousands of other Pakistani businesses — actually work. It was either too complex, too expensive, or required an internet connection we couldn't always rely on.

So we built Ledgr. An offline-first, beautifully simple finance ledger that runs on any Android phone, tracks money exactly how our community thinks about it, and exports to the formats our accountants actually ask for.

Today, Ledgr is used by shopkeepers, freelancers, families, and small businesses across Pakistan — all who want fast, private, and accurate bookkeeping without the noise.
