FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Ledgr really free?

Yes. Ledgr is completely free to download and use. If you'd like to support development, you can buy us a coffee from inside the app.

Is my data synced to the cloud?

No. Ledgr is fully offline. Your data stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. You can export it as Excel, CSV, or PDF whenever you want.

How do I backup my data?

Open Settings → Export to save statements as CSV, Excel, or PDF. Keep the file safe — you can re-import it manually if needed.

What does "balance before / after" mean?

Every entry stores your account balance the instant before and after that transaction. This creates a full audit trail so you can trace any discrepancy back to its source.

Does it support transfers between accounts?

Yes — just like Easypaisa, JazzCash, or a bank transfer. The source account is debited and the destination account is credited in one atomic transaction.

Which languages are supported?

English, Urdu (اردو), and Arabic (العربية), with full right-to-left layout support.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Ledgr stores data locally, so each device has its own independent database. To transfer data, use the export feature.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us at info@ashfaqalizardaristore.co or call +92 330 8060604. We read every message.
