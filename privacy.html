Privacy Policy

Last updated: . This policy explains how Ledgr handles your data.

1. Our commitment

Ledgr is developed and published by Ashfaq Ali Zardari Store ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the Ledgr mobile application collects (in short: nothing) and how your data is stored and used.

2. What we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Ledgr does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app. The app does not transmit your financial data, transaction history, or any other information to our servers or any third party.

3. How your data is stored

All data you enter into Ledgr — accounts, counterparties, transactions, notes, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device in a local database. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.

4. Exports and sharing

Ledgr allows you to export your data as Excel, CSV, or PDF files and share them via the system share sheet (e.g., WhatsApp, email, cloud storage). These exports are initiated entirely by you. Once a file leaves your device, its handling depends on the destination service's own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

Ledgr may request the following permissions:

  • Storage / Files — only to save export files to your device or to read backup files.
  • Camera / Photos — only if you choose to attach a receipt or avatar.
  • Biometric / Face unlock — optional, to lock the app.

We never access these permissions without your explicit action.

6. Third-party services

Ledgr does not embed advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social tracking. We do not use Firebase, Google Analytics, or similar services inside the app.

7. Children's privacy

Ledgr is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Data deletion

You can delete any record inside the app at any time. Uninstalling Ledgr removes the local database entirely from your device.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
