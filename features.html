Features

Built to handle real-world accounting.

Every feature in Ledgr was designed around a specific pain point shopkeepers and small businesses face daily.

👥

Accounts for People, Organizations & Companies

Create unlimited accounts and tag each as a Person, Organization, or Company. Give each a name, opening balance, currency, notes, and avatar. Archive accounts you no longer use without losing history.

  • Unlimited accounts
  • Full edit / archive / restore control
  • Designated "Main Account" for quick access
  • Per-account credit & debit totals
📈

Balance Before & After Every Entry

Ledgr's core engine snapshots your account balance the instant before and after every transaction. Audit trails become trivial. If a number doesn't add up, one tap shows you exactly where it drifted.

  • Automatic balance snapshots
  • Full audit trail per entry
  • Reversal-safe deletion
⇄

Income, Expense & Transfer Modes

Record money coming in, going out, or moving between your own accounts — just like Easypaisa, JazzCash, or a bank transfer. Transfers debit the source and credit the destination in a single atomic operation.

  • Income → credit selected account
  • Expense → debit selected account
  • Transfer → double-entry debit + credit
  • Optional counterparty linkage
📄

Export to Excel, CSV, and PDF

Generate clean, formatted statements for any account or date range. Excel files come with styled headers, CSV with UTF-8 BOM for full Excel compatibility, and PDFs with your company letterhead.

  • Styled Excel with autofilter
  • UTF-8 CSV (Excel-ready)
  • Professional PDF statements
  • Share via WhatsApp, email, or save
🔎

Powerful Filters & Search

Find any entry in seconds. Filter by date range, account, amount range, transaction type, or free-text search across descriptions and reference numbers.

  • Date range picker
  • Min / Max amount filter
  • Account & type filters
  • Full-text search
🎨

Themes & Multi-Language Support

Switch between light, dark, or system theme in a tap. The entire app is translated for English, Urdu (اردو), and Arabic (العربية) — with full RTL support.

  • Light / Dark / System themes
  • English, Urdu, Arabic
  • RTL layout for Urdu & Arabic
🔒

Privacy First — 100% Offline

Ledgr does not connect to any server. No signup, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. Your financial data lives only on your device.

  • No internet required
  • No account or sign-up
  • No third-party trackers
📊

Reports & Account-Wise Totals

See at a glance: total credits, total debits, net balance, and entry counts for every account. Compare monthly trends with beautiful charts.

  • Monthly credit vs debit chart
  • Account-wise summary table
  • Date-range reports
